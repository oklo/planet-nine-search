        Reading the map

        Purple shading is the density of 21,740 surviving reference orbits at the 2018 reference epoch, smoothed by 2°. Right ascension increases to the left; the ecliptic uses J2000.

        Outlines are sector image footprints, including overlapping and unsearched pixels. The faintest green marks an assessed field with no demonstrated sensitivity. Stronger shading reflects recovery of injected test planets.

        Zooming into a field shows the faintest recovered injection within the field. An "unresolved flag" is a trial trajectory that exceeded the field’s control scores. Several trajectories can flag the same residual, so this is not a count of distinct objects. Hollow green squares mark fields with open traces. A dash means flags were not assessed.

        Flags are screened against the local orbital and physical ranges of the Brown reference population, with allowances for sampling and measurement uncertainty. Rejected trials remain in the historical record. These classifications are conditional on the adopted planet-nine population.

        Missed injections and uncalibrated depths are marked explicitly. T denotes TESS magnitude and V visual magnitude. Click a field for recovery counts at each tested magnitude.
